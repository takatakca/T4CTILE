## 1. Principle: order data only

TAKATAK — Commande tactile is an ordering interface for touchscreens connected to the merchant's Clover account. **No customer account is required to order (guest mode).** The App processes only the data of the order itself, described below.

## 2. Information processed

To place and complete your order, the App processes:

- **Order content** — items selected, quantities, options and modifiers, prices and taxes.
- **Transaction data generated by Clover** — order number, time, total amount, payment status.
- **Where applicable, a phone number** — only if you voluntarily identify yourself on screen (for example, for a separate loyalty program such as TAKATAK Fidélité). Without that identification, no number is requested or retained by the App.

The App **does not collect or see your payment card numbers**: payment is processed directly by the Clover terminal paired with the screen and by its gateway. The App records neither image nor sound: no camera or microphone is used for ordering.

## 3. Why we process this data

- Compose and transmit your order to the merchant (counter and kitchen)
- Calculate totals and taxes from the merchant's menu and rates
- Match the completed payment to the correct order and display the confirmation
- Allow the merchant to view and manage its orders in Clover

We do **not** use order data for advertising purposes, and we **never sell** personal information.

## 4. Disclosure and hosting

- Orders and payments are processed in Clover (Fiserv) systems, at the merchant. The App reads the merchant's menu and writes orders into the merchant's Clover account.
- Data may be hosted or processed in Canada or the United States depending on the Clover infrastructure used; it is protected by the measures in Section 6 and by providers' contractual obligations.
- No disclosure to other third parties, except where required by law.

## 5. Retention

Orders form part of the merchant's business records in Clover and are retained according to the merchant's own obligations (invoicing, taxes, accounting). TAKATAK does not keep a separate copy of order content beyond what is necessary to operate and troubleshoot the App (short-term technical logs). The touchscreen resets after each order: no data from the previous order is shown to the next customer.

## 6. Security

Safeguards applied: encrypted communications (HTTPS/TLS), access authentication through Clover mechanisms (OAuth / per-merchant access tokens), restricted administrative access, and logging. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious harm, we will notify the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec and the individuals concerned, in accordance with Law 25.
